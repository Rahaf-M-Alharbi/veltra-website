About Veltra

An MEP contractor sized to its projects

Founded in Riyadh in 2013, with one responsible engineer accountable for every project through to handover.

CompanySince 2013Rev. R2
01 Who we are

One team, one engineer accountable

Veltra was founded in Riyadh in 2013 and works today with government entities, main contractors and developers in healthcare, commercial and education.

Each project is led by one responsible engineer from mobilisation to handover, who sends a weekly progress report to the owner and consultant.

We follow Civil Defense requirements and the Saudi Building Code, and hand over as-built drawings and O&M manuals with every project.
